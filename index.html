Code first

I’m Ahmad, a software engineering student at the Libyan International Medical University, and I build software for real businesses. Most of them are my own.

My family has run Mufti Gallery since the 1950s. As its CEO I’m turning a room of antiques and fine art into a bilingual online auction house, which is also my graduation project.

I joined Pulsar as an intern in January 2026 and now work there as a security engineer. It changed how I write code: I assume someone is trying to break it.

I also founded TechGuard, which installs Hikvision camera systems in homes and businesses, and DARA MAN, a resale label for designer menswear.
